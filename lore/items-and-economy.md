# Items, Materials & The Economy of Essence

## Material Classification

Every material in Ashenmoor carries inherent **traits** — properties imparted by essence saturation during the material's formation. These traits are not arbitrary labels; they represent real physical and arcane characteristics that determine how a material behaves when subjected to Crucible synthesis.

### The Seven Traits

| Trait | Description | Practical Effect |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Density** | Molecular compression from geological essence pressure | Creates heavy, durable outputs. Foundation of all structural materials. |
| **Sharpness** | Crystalline microstructure formation at molecular level | Produces cutting edges, piercing points. Essential for weaponry. |
| **Organic** | Biological essence integration — the material was or is alive | Enables healing, growth, and biological interaction. |
| **Toxicity** | Concentrated, unstable essence that resists metabolization | Produces poisons, corrosives, and volatile compounds. |
| **Magic** | Purified essence in a stable, channelable form | Powers enchantments, wards, and energy storage. The rarest and most valuable trait. |
| **Liquid** | fluid-state essence binding | Enables dissolution, absorption, and mixture. Carrier trait for other properties. |
| **None** | No significant essence imprint | Inert filler. Stabilizes otherwise volatile combinations. |

### Why Traits Matter

When three materials are combined in the Crucible, their traits **interact**. The synthesis engine evaluates which traits dominate, which cancel, and which combine to produce emergent properties. This is why alchemists obsess over trait combinations — the difference between a "Heavy Iron Ingot" and an "Eldritch Blade Shard" is not the materials themselves, but the *ratios and intersections* of their traits.

## Base Materials Registry

### Minerals (Mined from the Deep Mines)

**Stone**
- Traits: Density / None
- *The most common material. Foundation stone for construction, filler material for alchemy. Worthless individually, essential in bulk. Every settlement consumes stone constantly.*

**Copper Ore**
- Traits: Density / None
- *More refined than raw stone but still considered a base metal. Copper's main value is in alloy creation — combined with essence-rich materials, it gains properties far beyond its humble origins. The Coin itself is pressed copper alloy.*

**Iron Ore**
- Traits: Density / Sharpness
- *The workhorse of the industrial economy. Iron from essence-veins naturally develops a crystalline microstructure that makes it harder and sharper than surface iron. Deep-vein iron is the raw material for all serious weaponry.*

**Ruby**
- Traits: Magic / Density
- *Rubies form deep underground where essence concentration is highest. They are natural essence batteries — capable of storing and slowly releasing arcane energy. A single high-quality ruby can power a ward for years. Extremely valuable. Extremely dangerous to mine.*

### Timber (Harvested from the Whispering Woods)

**Oak Wood**
- Traits: Organic / Density
- *Dense, essence-saturated hardwood. The Whispering Woods' oaks have root networks that channel essence across kilometers. Cut oak retains this channeling property, making it useful for structural alchemy and essence-conductive frameworks.*

**Spruce Wood**
- Traits: Organic / None
- *Lighter and more flexible than oak. Spruce absorbs essence passively but doesn't retain it strongly. Primarily used as a stabilizing element in alchemy — its low essence retention prevents volatile reactions.*

### Biological Materials (Gathered or Harvested)

**Red Herb**
- Traits: Organic / Magic
- *Grows in clearings within the Whispering Woods where essence pools in the soil. The only naturally-occurring plant that combines organic growth with stable magical essence. Therapists use processed Red Herb as the base for most healing compounds. Alchemists prize it for its dual-trait versatility.*

**Wolf Tooth**
- Traits: Sharpness / Organic
- *Dropped by the crystalline-toothed wolves of the Whispering Woods. Generations of essence exposure have turned their teeth into a natural composite material — organically grown but with crystalline sharpness rivaling forged metal. Alchemists favor them for any recipe requiring cutting or piercing properties with biological compatibility.*

## The Economy by the Numbers

### Why Coins Matter

The entire economy of Ashenmoor revolves around a single truth: **everything essential costs coin, and nothing essential is free.**

| Need | Cost | Why It's Expensive |
|------|------|--------------------|
| Healing (per HP) | 5 coins | Therapist monopoly + essence-herb supply chain |
| Stash Upgrade (20→50) | 5,000 coins | Real estate within walls is finite and taxed |
| Stash Upgrade (50→100) | 25,000 coins | Larger spaces require Garrison-grade security |
| Stash Upgrade (100→200) | 100,000 coins | Reserved for the wealthiest. A statement of power. |
| Equipment | Varies | Guild smiths charge based on material quality |

### The Greed Spiral

1. You need **coin** to survive (healing, storage, equipment).
2. You need **materials** to earn coin (sell to merchants, complete quests).
3. You need **expeditions** to gather materials (risk life and gear).
4. Expeditions **cost** health (requiring healing → more coin).
5. Materials **take space** (requiring stash upgrades → more coin).
6. Better expeditions need **better gear** (requiring crafting → more materials → more expeditions).

The system is self-sustaining because it's self-consuming. There is no equilibrium — only escalation.

## Crucible Alchemy in Practice

### How Discovery Works

No recipe is known in advance. The first person to combine a specific set of three materials discovers whatever the Crucible produces. Their name is attached to the discovery forever. This creates:

- **Race dynamics**: Multiple alchemists independently trying common combinations, hoping to be first.
- **Information asymmetry**: The discoverer knows the recipe. Everyone else is guessing.
- **Underground trade**: Recipes are bought, sold, stolen, and counterfeited. A proven recipe for a healing salve is worth thousands of coins.

### Why Failure Punishes

When a combination lacks coherent trait interaction, the Crucible produces **Volatile Ash** — inert, worthless residue. The materials are consumed. The coin spent on those materials is gone. The stash space they occupied is freed, which is the only mercy.

This ensures that alchemy is never a guaranteed income source. It's gambling with your inventory.
