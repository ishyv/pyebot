# Tone Guide & AI Prompt Reference

This document defines the narrative voice for all AI-generated content, bot embeds, and flavor text in the RPG system.

---

## Setting Identity

**Ashenmoor** is a Dark Victorian / Lovecraftian world with grounded realism inspired by:
- **Elden Ring**: The oppressive, decayed grandeur. The sense that civilization is built on ruins it doesn't fully understand.
- **Bloodborne**: Gothic architecture, medical horror, the thin line between science and madness.
- **Mushoku Tensei**: The mundane realism beneath the fantasy. People eat, work, suffer, and die. Economics matter. Social class matters. Nobody is special by default.

## Voice Rules

### DO
- Write like a worn leather journal entry. Terse. Pragmatic. Observational.
- Use concrete sensory details: "*The ore smelled of copper and ozone.*"
- Let horror be implied, not stated: "*The tunnel went deeper than the map showed. It always does.*"
- Acknowledge that the characters are ordinary people in extraordinary circumstances.
- Use economic language naturally: cost, debt, margin, yield, supply.

### DON'T
- Use epic fantasy bombast: no "mighty heroes," no "ancient prophecies," no "chosen ones."
- Use modern slang or anachronisms.
- Make anything feel safe or whimsical. Even humor should be gallows humor.
- Over-explain the magic system. People in-world use essence the way we use electricity — they don't monologue about how it works.

## AI System Prompt (for Crucible Name Generation)

Use this as the system prompt when calling the AI to generate Crucible synthesis item names:

```
You are a grim, arcane alchemist in a Dark Victorian / Lovecraftian universe called Ashenmoor — a world of permanent grey skies, essence-infused materials, and desperate survival economies. The tone is Elden Ring meets Bloodborne meets grounded realism.

When naming synthesized items:
- Use 2-4 word noun phrases.
- Draw from Victorian scientific vocabulary, occult terminology, and industrial-age metallurgy.
- Names should feel like they belong in an alchemist's handwritten catalog.
- Avoid generic fantasy ("Sword of Light", "Magic Potion"). Prefer specificity ("Calcified Marrow Shard", "Distilled Grey Tincture").
```

## Embed Flavor Text Examples

### Expedition Start
> *The gate warden doesn't wish you luck. He stopped doing that years ago.*

### Push Deeper
> *Something scrapes against stone in the darkness ahead. Could be wind. Could be teeth.*

### Successful Extraction
> *You emerge into grey light, lungs burning, pack heavy. The walls have never looked so welcoming.*

### Death
> *The cold finds you before the dark does. Your gear scatters across the tunnel floor. Someone else will find it. Someone always does.*

### Crucible — New Discovery
> *The Crucible shudders. The compound stabilizes. You've made something no one has made before. Write it down before you forget how.*

### Crucible — Failure (Volatile Ash)
> *A flash of heat. A smell like burning hair. What remains in the vessel is grey powder and wasted potential.*

### Healing at the Therapist
> *The Therapist works quickly and without sympathy. Needle, thread, salve. The bill arrives before the bleeding stops.*

### Stash Upgrade
> *The clerk stamps your new allocation. More space. More to lose.*
